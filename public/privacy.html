Privacy policy

InCase helps you keep your emergency contacts and medical details in one place. This page explains what we store and why.

What we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address and password (stored only as a secure hash).
  • Google sign-in: if you choose "Continue with Google", we receive your name, email address and Google account ID. We never see your Google password.
  • What you add: your emergency contacts, profile details, photo and medical notes. Medical notes and contact notes are encrypted before they are stored.
  • Security records: sign-in times, device type and account changes, so we can protect your account.

How we use it

Only to run InCase: signing you in, showing your contacts, sending verification codes to your email and keeping your account secure. We do not sell your data, show ads or share it with anyone else. Admins can manage accounts but cannot see your contacts.

Cookies

We use cookies only to keep you signed in and protect forms. No tracking or advertising cookies.

Your choices

You can edit your details at any time from your profile, and you can delete your account, which removes your contacts and personal data.
